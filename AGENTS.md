# cb_site

- This repository owns the Conch's Blessing codex UI and Pages workflow. Mod definitions and translations come from the checked-out `Ba-koD/conch_blessing` source through `generate_items_js.py`.
- Preserve a focused search input across filtering. During IME composition, wait for composition completion and update only the result rows; never recreate the detail panel per keystroke.
- The filter button beside the search opens a panel of extra conditions: type, quality, item pool, stacking, golden/Mom's Box (trinkets), and features (own upgrade effect, synergies, evolution). Choices within one group widen the match, groups narrow it, and the feature group needs every chosen feature. Each option shows how many items it covers, and options no item has are left out. Like the search and the answer chips, filters only dim tiles in place, and a chip changes in place so it keeps focus; the button shows how many choices are active, and Escape closes the panel. Do not offer a REPENTOGON filter derived from EID warnings, because some items that need it do not say so in their EID.
- Synergy filters search both English/Korean names and synergy descriptions. Normalize whitespace and match every query term across those fields, including mixed name/description queries.
- Synergies are the last content section in item details, after effects, upgrade paths, facts, and link actions, on both desktop and mobile.
- Show Magic Conch origin/answer/result mappings for mod origins as well as vanilla origins. Evolution routes and their localized conditions are separate, derived only from explicit evolution statements with own-item references in source locales; neither an origin mapping nor a downgrade establishes evolution.
- Detail previews have a viewport-aware maximum height and internal scrolling, and shrink for short content on desktop and mobile. An explicitly pinned desktop item wins over hover/focus previews until unpinned or another item is selected; clicking its tile again unpins it.
- Every collectible, familiar, active and trinket detail lists whether copies stack, from the mod registry's `stackable` field (absent means it does not; actives never stack). For a trinket this counts more copies only. Golden copies and Mom's Box are a separate fact shown on every trinket (user decision 2026-10-29), because a trinket may stack yet ignore them or the reverse (Angel's Crown: only golden copies and Mom's Box change it). Its 황금·엄마의 상자 effect section, right after the effects, comes from the trinket's EID `specials`: the `append` lines in golden / Mom's Box / both order, or the effect lines with the `specials.normal` numbers multiplied x2 for golden or Mom's Box and x3 for both, as the engine's trinket multiplier does; golden and Mom's Box share one row when they read the same. Effect text drops EID icon markup, except that a `{{QualityN}}` icon stands in for its grade and becomes the digit N ("0/1등급"). Conch Pearl leaves out held items that do not stack, so this fact is how players see which ones it can still give. Cards and runes have no stacking fact.
- Vanilla item IDs, English/Korean names, and sprite paths belong in `data/vanilla_items.json`. `import_vanilla_items.py` reads extracted vanilla XML/string tables and installed enums without modifying the game files or another mod. Preserve native pixel sprites under `assets/vanilla`; do not invent Korean translations from enum names.
- `build_vanilla_js.py` validates the committed catalog/assets before export. Pages packages that export and the full vanilla asset directory. All vanilla references use their ID/type namespace to avoid collectible/trinket collisions.
- Cards and runes come from the mod's pure-data `scripts/items/cards/definitions.lua` and their `pockets.<KEY>` locale text. They are additions to the game, not Magic Conch results: show them in their own group with a how-to-get note and no upgrade path. `generate_items_js.py` crops each one's pocket-slot art (the third 32x32 frame of its sheet) into `site_pockets/`, which the Pages workflow publishes as `resources/gfx/items/pockets/`.
- Every Magic Conch upgrade plays the mod's default animation, so only an item's own presentation counts as having one (user decision 2026-10-29): a catalog row with an `effect` (`fx: "applied"`) is labelled 전용 강화 연출 / Own upgrade effect, and a pending concept (`fx: "pending"`) shows no player in details and is left out of that feature filter. The effects tab is the mod's own player and still lists the concepts.
- Item details show an item's own upgrade presentation directly through the player's compact mode (`upgrade-player.html?embed=1#KEY`), as its own section right after the upgrade path; it waits on a still frame and plays when pressed, never by switching to the effects tab. Each detail view (desktop panel, mobile sheet) keeps one player for good: re-renders and hover/focus previews only send it a `show` message, so it never reloads the 14 MB page or adds history entries, and the player is created lazily the first time a view shows an item with an effect. Size each small player from the height it reports, and stop a player the page hides (the sheet's when it closes, small ones when the effects tab opens, the tab's when the codex returns). Volume belongs to the player and is shared between players through localStorage.
- Before publishing, check generation against the current mod source, image/reference coverage, Korean composition events without input replacement, English/Korean search, desktop/mobile layout, and the Pages workflow result.
